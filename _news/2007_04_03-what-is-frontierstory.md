## Welcome to FrontierStory

**FrontierStory** is a **free‑to‑play, 2D side‑scrolling MMORPG** where you embark on an adventure in a vibrant, whimsical world. Create your hero and explore vast lands, fight monsters, and uncover hidden treasures.

### Choose your class

You start as an adventurer and can specialize into one of **five iconic classes**:

| Class | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Warrior** | Dominate the frontlines|
| **Magician** | Cast powerful spells with magic|
| **Archer** | Strike from afar with either precision or speed |
| **Thief** | Strike from the shadows with high critical rate |
| **Gunslinger** | Can engage the enemies at both range and melee|

Will you be the one taking hits for the team, or the shadow that eliminates enemies before they react? **You choose.**

## The Economy

FrontierStory uses two currencies:

| Currency | Use | Conversion |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| **Yen** | In‑game currency – drops from monsters, quest rewards, selling to NPCs. Used for buying potions, basic gear, etc. | Not convertible to real money officially |
| **Credits** | Player‑to‑player real‑money currency. **1000 Credits = 1 USD**. Used for trading rare items between players outside the official systems. | Via PayPal, gift cards, etc. |

The **only official way** to obtain Credits is through the in‑game Cash Shop: **$10 USD = 10,000 Credits**.

## Trading & Scam Warning

- **Real‑money trading (RMT)** is common but **against the game's ToS**. GMs may ban offenders.
- The forum does **not** provide escrow. Trade at your own risk.
- Typical scam: a trusted user builds reputation with small trades, then disappears with high‑value items.
- **If scammed**, game GMs won't help. Your only options are PayPal dispute or community tracking.

## RULES

1. **Trading Yen for Credits is NOT ALLOWED** under the official game rules.
2. All trades not supported by the official system (including player‑to‑player Credit‑for‑item trades) are done **entirely at the player's own risk**. The forum, game developers, and moderators assume no responsibility for lost items or currency.
