Hello, FrontierStory adventurers!

We are announcing the third job advancement. The 3rd job advancemenr occurs at level 70, in which you will be able to begin a quest. To begin the quest, both strengths and wits are required.

1.Go to your job instructor 
2. Head to the Chief Residence in Tierra Fria
3. You will be tasked to look for the door of dimension, a powerful foe awaits.
4. Afterwards, you will be need to head to the Holy Stone, where over there you will need to answer some questions.
5. Return to the Chief Residence and complete your ascension!
